# Intent-Aware Continuity in Evolving AI Systems

*A Typed, Provenance-Aware Framework for Continuity Judgments in AI Governance*
Matthew A. Davis, 2026.

## What it contains
Complete manuscript source, all experiment code, and one-command regeneration of every
figure and every number quoted in the paper. Experiments are seeded and deterministic
for a fixed NumPy version.

## Scope and limits
The repository ships a `LIMITATIONS.md` stating what the framework has **not** been shown
to do — including that it has not been exercised in a live release-governance workflow.
Read it before citing the work as validated practice.

Version 5.0 corrected two methodology errors present in v4.1, both documented in the
repository's `CHANGELOG.md` rather than silently revised.
